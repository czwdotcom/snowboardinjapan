    How to export from Slopes
    1. In Slopes, open Logbook and tap the day.
    2. Tap the ⋯ below the date and location → Export As → GPX. Pick the full-day or raw GPS option so lift rides and queues are included.
    3. Save it to Files, come back here and tap Import from Slopes.
